# Northstar demo support handbook
This is fictional public demonstration data.
## Contact
Standard support is available Monday through Friday.
Support hours are 09:00 to 17:00 UTC.
Critical incidents can be escalated to the on-call engineer.

## Billing
Refund requests must be submitted within fourteen days.
Refunds are processed through the original payment method.
Annual plans include a twenty percent discount.
Invoices are available from the billing dashboard.

## Maintenance
Scheduled maintenance occurs on the first Sunday of each month.
The maintenance window starts at 02:00 UTC.
Customers receive maintenance notices forty-eight hours in advance.
